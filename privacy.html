Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Who we are

"Deep House Chillout Music" is a YouTube channel (@deephouse.chillout). Its owner runs private studio software that uploads and manages the channel's own videos through the official YouTube API Services.

What the software accesses

The software signs in with Google only to the channel owner's own account. With the permissions granted by the owner it can upload videos, set thumbnails and playlists, update the channel's description and branding, post the channel's own comments, and read the channel's YouTube Analytics reports. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.

Data we store

  • An OAuth access token, stored only on the owner's own computer or server, used to act on the owner's channel.
  • Aggregated statistics of the channel's own videos (views, watch time, retention, subscribers gained) used to improve future videos.
  • Public metadata of public YouTube videos (titles, durations, view counts, thumbnails) retrieved for market analysis.

We do not collect personal data of viewers, do not sell or share any data, and do not use data for advertising.

Google and YouTube

This software uses YouTube API Services. By watching our videos you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The owner can revoke the software's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; stored tokens are then deleted.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while access is granted. Analytics summaries are kept as long as needed to improve the channel and can be deleted on request.

Contact

Questions about this policy: via the contact options on the YouTube channel page.
